# Proposed later assessment, not launched

This is a small artifact-usefulness decision, not a continuation of R6. A fresh independent review must settle its feasibility and the user must authorize the new live scope before launch. If that scope is not approved, stop at the static candidate.

## Decision and fixed design

Decide whether v2 earns a limited, supervised user trial over the frozen [concise template](../r6/simple-template.txt) or direct task execution. Do not compare v1 again on its known diagnostic and call that fresh evidence.

- Two new authoring briefs: one repeatable local batch-file transformation that is not the R6 CSV task; one source-grounded draft/update workflow with a pre-existing draft and a requirement to preserve unaffected content. Use synthetic, publishable data. Freeze exact briefs, outputs, permitted effects and checks before admissions.
- For each family, v2 and the exact R6 template each get one authoring slot and produce one selected package. The direct policy gets no additional task-specific package. All use the same available host/capability envelope, without a bare-model or native-system claim. No paid service, installation or external side effect.
- Three consumer uses per family per policy: first supported use, a changed-input second use of the identical frozen package, and the relevant blocked boundary. That is four author slots and eighteen consumer slots, with twelve positive-artifact opportunities and six boundary opportunities. Keep every assignment, timeout and missing artifact in its fixed denominator; no best-of selection, replacement generation or selective rerun.
- The file contract states absent versus existing-empty destinations, exact occupied targets, and permitted path boundaries. First use includes an authorized existing directory with absent targets; second use changes input and authorized destination without package edits. The blocked use has an occupied unauthorized target or unsafe resolved path. The draft workflow specifies existing content, allowed edits, new evidence and its missing-authority boundary. Correct refusal is the expected boundary result.

The directory pattern and collision pattern are known regression mechanisms. New workflow briefs and changed-input instances extend coverage, but are not independent holdouts when the same team authors and reviews them. Authors receive the full supported-state contract and development example, not hidden restrictions. Keep final consumer inputs out of their authoring context; disclose shared readable storage if it remains. Freeze outputs/checks before exposure and label any later tuning as development, requiring new cases for another assessment.

The repeated-use benefit sought is a correct second use without package edits or new user clarification/repair. Report all first/second-use outcomes, boundary outcomes and observed authoring/usage effort separately. A provisional preference for v2 requires successful first and second use in both families, no observed boundary failure, and fewer second-use clarification/repair needs than the simpler policies, with no extra output failures. A tie, missing evidence or a cost tradeoff yields no preference or a user decision. These are proposed practical criteria, not a statistical margin or a proof of amortized savings. A capable direct solution or template may win.

## Feasible supervision and records

Use one substantive worker at a time. Proposed allowance: USD 0 new external spend; at most 22 fresh admissions, no resumes or child delegation; eight minutes per author and two per consumer, measured as observed wall time. Allow 120 minutes total from preparation start through publication, with at most 15 minutes preparation and a 90-minute stop on new experimental work, leaving 30 minutes for reconciliation and publication. If interruption cannot be confirmed, do not start the next worker. Stop early for an authority/evidence blocker; retain the remainder as unrun. Declare any actual overshoot instead of promising perfect enforcement.

Instruct ceilings of sixteen tool calls per author and ten per consumer, but classify these as self-reported unless the host exposes authoritative counters. Do not make a verified call-cap or cost claim from them. Record exact serving model, tokens, global calls and active time only if exposed; otherwise unknown. If this control scheme is infeasible in the selected host, revise and freeze the plan before launch rather than improvise during it. No expansion until a preferred result appears.

Keep a compact per-slot record: assigned policy/input and starting-state identity (including relevant directories), admission/completion observations, selected package version, package-produced flag, report-completed flag, budget observability, actual output-producing commands/scripts and their results, output bytes, all failures and known interventions. Capture returned command text as attributed when it is not host-observed. Bound permissions and safety claims by action coverage; final files alone do not prove authorized action history.

Agree on evidence disposition before launch. Public synthetic briefs, authored package sources, curated commands/results and output bytes may be proposed for the existing research branch; exclude secrets, private host instructions and unrelated context. Confirm a supported local retention destination. No raw host archive or formerly denied R6 payload may be uploaded, retried or encoded into another channel. If the agreed useful evidence cannot be retained, stop rather than manufacture provenance. A reviewer can judge source-grounded outputs against bound source/output bytes; accept equivalent valid organization and report unresolved disagreement. No new semantic-reviewer campaign is part of these 22 slots.
